01 / THE COMPETITION

What is STEM Racing?

STEM Racing is an international competition where school teams design, build, test, market and race miniature Formula 1 cars. It brings science, technology, engineering and mathematics to life, while developing teamwork, creativity, enterprise and real-world problem-solving.

04 / OUR CAR

NIGHTFALL

Where daylight ends and darkness begins…

Every surface, contour and component on our car has a job to do. The result is a refined design built to reduce drag, manage stability and make every millisecond count.

HOURS DEVELOPING

ENGINEERING

Optimisation

We focused on speed and stability through aerodynamic refinement. Nightfall’s geometry was developed with a focus on reducing drag while maintaining the stability needed for consistent performance. We used Simscale/CFD to further identify areas for improvement and refine the car’s profile.

Build precisely

Turning a digital design into a precise physical car. Our manufacturing process focuses on accuracy at every stage, from machining the body to finishing the final surfaces. We use measured checks throughout the build to ensure the finished car stays as close as possible to our intended design.

How we know it works

Testing, analysing and refining every iteration. We didn’t just design once and simply manufacture. We used testing and analysis to identify weaknesses, make informed changes and evaluate each iteration. The final design is the result of repeatedly feeding what we learned back into the engineering process.
